downeast-maine-pumpkin-bread

Description

	This pumpkin bread is a great old Main recipe, moist and fill of warm spices. The bread actually tastes even better the day after it is 
	baked. It's easy to make with canned pumpkin and perfect for holiday gift-giving!

Ingredients

  • Pumpkin puree
- 1(15-ounce) can pumpkin puree

  • Large egss
- 4 large eggs

  • Vegetable oil
- 1 cup vegetable oil

  • Water
- 2/3 cup water

  • White sugar
- 3 cups white sugar

  • All-purpose flour
- 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

  • Baking soda
- 2 teaspoons baking soda

  • Salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt

  • Ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

  • Ground nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg

  • Ground cloves
- 1 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves

  • Ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger

Directions

  1. Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease and flour two 9x5-inch
loaf pans.

  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and ginger together in a large bowl.

  3. Whisk pumpkin puree, eggs, oil, water, and sugar together in a seperate large bowl until smooth and well
combined.

  4. Stir the flour mixture into the pumpkin mixture just until no dry streaks remain; do not overmix.

  5. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared loaf pans.

  6. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of each loaf comes out clean or with a
few moist crumbs, about 50 minutes.

  7. Cool the loaves in their pans for 10 minutes. Run a thin knife around the edges, then turn the loaves onto a
wire rack to cool completely before slicing or wrapping.
