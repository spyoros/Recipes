Sausage, Peppers, Onions, and Potato Bake

Description

	This sausage and potato bake is an italian comfort food classic. The smell of this cooking in my kitchen transports me back to playing 
	on the street in front of my Papa and gigi's house in brookyln. I make 2 trays of this for every family get-together, and its gone in a
	flash! If you're looking for a comforting meal that is made with ingredients you currently have in the fridge, then you've found the right
	recipe for you. This recipe with sausage, potatos, onion, and peppers starts out in the skillet, but turns into a bubbly casserole-like
	dish after a trip to the oven.

Ingredients

  • Olieve oil
The olieve oil is used for cooking your sausage, potatoes, and veggies in the skillet.
- 2 teaspoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup olive oil

  • Italian Sausage
Choose your favorite Italian sausage (mild, medium, or got will work) for this recipe.
- 2 pounds Italian sausage links, cut into 2-inch pieces

  • Potatoes
You can use whatever potatoes you like, as long as you peel and slice them. but for faster
cooking and less chopping, try smaller red potatoes or flingerlings so you can eat them whole.
- 4 large potatoes, peeled and thickly sliced

  • Bell Peppers
Both red and green bell peppers are used in this recipe - and, hey, if you have yellow or
orange, throw those in too!
- 2 Large green bell peppers, seeded and cut into wedges
- 2 large red bell peppers, seeded and cut into wedges

  • Onions
Yellow onions will work bes there for their caramelized flavor.
- 3 large onions, cut into wedges

  • White Wine
White wine adds flavor to the veggies and sausage. Plus, it keeps the dish moist during baking.
- 1/2 cup white wine

  • Chicken Stock
This recipe uses chicken stock, though you can use any sotck you have on hand in a pinch.
- 1/2 cup chicken stock

  • Seasonings
Lightly season the sausage and vegetables with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- salt and fresly ground black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.

  2. Preheat the oven to 400 degress F (200 degrees C).

  3. Heat 2 teaspons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, and cook and stir the sausage until
browned, 5 to 10 minutes. Transfer cooked sausage to a large baking dish.

  4. Pour 1/4 cup of olive oil into the skillet, and cook potatoes, stirring occasionally, until browned,
about 10 minutes.

  5. Place the potatoes into the baking dish, leaving some oil in the skillet.

  6. Cook and stir green and red peppers and onions in the hot skillet until they are beginning to soften,
about 5 minutes.

  7. Add the vegetables to the baking dish. Pour wine and chicken stock over the vegetables and sausage, and
sprinkle with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.

  8. Gently stir sausage, potatoes, and vegetables together.

  9. Bake in the preheated oven until hot and bubbling, 20 to 25 minutes. Serve hot.
